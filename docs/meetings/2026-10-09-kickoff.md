## Scope

SitWise watches posture, screen distance, blinks and fatigue with a normal webcam. It is a desktop app. Camera frames stay in memory on the user's computer. The server receives numeric summaries only.

Roadmap v2, dated 21 October 2026, is the plan from this note forward. Models are trained on numbers taken from MediaPipe landmarks, including the team's own SitWise-Desk set. A model runs on the user's computer. The final report compares four approaches on people the model has not seen: rule thresholds, a public-data model, the SitWise-Desk model, and a personalized threshold.

Out of scope: saving image or video, training a neural net on images, a mobile app, and medical diagnosis.

## Model and dataset

The dataset stores no image. Each row is an anonymous person code, a time, the feature numbers, a label, the ruler distance, camera type, glasses and light. Public sets (UTA-RLDD, Eyeblink8, a front-view posture set) are converted to the same columns and then not kept as video in the repo. The choice of each set is SW-069. The collection script and the consent form are SW-070.
